Watershed Role Playing Activity

Grades 5–9 · 60 min · Indoors or Outdoors
Download
Ecology and Ecosystems Water Quality Environmental Science MS-ESS3-3 Apply scientific principles to design a method for monitoring and minimizing human impact on the environment. MS-LS2-4 Construct an argument supported by empirical evidence that changes to physical or biological components of an ecosystem affect populations. MS-LS2-5 Evaluate competing design solutions for maintaining biodiversity and ecosystem services.

The goal of this program is to educate students about the importance of watersheds and how human activities can severely impact them. This is a group-centered role play activity where students take on the roles of different stakeholders to explore how individual and community decisions affect water quality and ecosystem health downstream.

River Ecosystems (w/ Camera Trap game)

Grades 3–7 · 60 min · Indoors
Download
Ecology and Ecosystems Wildlife ID Community Science 2-LS4-1 Make observations of plants and animals to compare the diversity of life in different habitats. 4-LS1-1 Construct an argument that plants and animals have internal and external structures that function to support survival, growth, behavior, and reproduction. MS-LS2-1 Analyze and interpret data to provide evidence for the effects of resource availability on organisms and populations of organisms in an ecosystem.

This presentation-based program is focused around educating students about river ecosystems, research done in rivers, and Illinois wildlife. Best-suited for middle school students, the program includes a general overview of the topics discussed above, as well as a game during which students group up with whiteboards and identify wildlife from camera trap images (images courtesy of Urban Rivers). Extra camera trap images included in zip.

Bat Echolocation: Acoustic Monitoring and ID

Grades 10+/ Adults · 60 min · Indoors
Download
Bats Wildlife ID Bioacoustics HS-PS4-1 Use mathematical representations to support a claim regarding relationships among frequency, wavelength, and speed of waves traveling in various media. HS-LS1-2 Develop and use a model to illustrate the hierarchical organization of interacting systems that provide specific functions within multicellular organisms. HS-LS2-6 Evaluate claims and reasoning that complex interactions in ecosystems maintain relatively consistent numbers and types of organisms in stable conditions.

The goal of this program is to educate students about Illinois bat species, echolocation, acoustic monitoring, and acoustic bat call identification. This is generally a higher level program meant for high school aged students or adults. The program is presentation-based and includes a general introduction to the afore-mentioned topics as well as a bat call identification game towards the end of the slides. A acoustic ID guide is also included to give as a handout during the game.

How to Lead a Bio-Blitz using iNaturalist

All Ages · Full-day · Outdoors
Download
Ecology and Ecosystems Community Science Wildlife ID 2-LS4-1 Make observations of plants and animals to compare the diversity of life in different habitats.

The goal of these materials is to give anyone (even someone new to iNaturalist) the required tools to not only lead a Bio-Blitz, but provide interesting results for the participants in the form of statistical graphs. This program includes instructions for project creation, instructions for how to make an iNaturalist observation (meant to be distributed to participants), example data from a bio-blitz I ran in 2025, and R code to generate plots directly from the iNaturalist output data.
